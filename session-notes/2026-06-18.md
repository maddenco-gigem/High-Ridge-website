## Notes

- **Legal stance**: US/Texas is an opt-out regime — running analytics/ad cookies by default is permitted with notice + accessible opt-out + GPC honoring. Opt-in is only required for EU/UK (out of scope). No dark patterns (manipulated Accept = invalid consent + deceptive). For an RIA, send **no PII/financial data** to ad platforms, and get CCO sign-off before production.
- **Headless `capture-pages.sh` cannot emulate mobile** — legacy `--headless` renders the desktop layout and clips it to the window, so it's invalid for mobile checks (the whole page overflows, not just our UI). Use Chrome DevTools device mode / a real device. Fixed-position overlays (banner, cookie notice) also don't reliably render in the tall full-page capture.
